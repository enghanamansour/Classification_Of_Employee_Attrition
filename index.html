Executive Retention Playbook

1. Mitigate Overtime Burnout

OverTime is the single highest predictor of employee departure (+1.59 log-odds). Cap consecutive weeks of overtime and automate routine tasks in high-churn departments.

2. Manager Continuity

Employees with a new manager (<1 year) exhibit 3.2x higher flight risk. Implement formal onboarding and mentorship check-ins during manager transitions.

3. Equity Attachment

Employees with Stock Option Level 0 have over 65% higher attrition. Broad-based vesting grants for key technical roles significantly increase retention stickiness.
